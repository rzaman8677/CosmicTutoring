Raiyan Zaman, President of Technology / Communications

Raiyan Zaman, an enthusiastic junior at Centennial High School in Ellicott City, harbors a profound interest in the realm of technology. A proficient programmer and web developer, he is tirelessly dedicated to expanding his knowledge in emerging tech fields, including AI, neural networks, and deep learning. Raiyan has already demonstrated his technical acumen through internships at two prestigious colleges, where he honed his skills in practical applications of technology. Currently, he is channeling his passion into an ambitious project: the construction of an autonomous robot.

At school, Raiyan leads with distinction as the president of two key clubs: the Web Development Club and DESCO, a CAD Club. His innovative approach and leadership skills contribute to the success of these groups. Moreover, he applies his tech-savviness as the webmaster of the Tech Journal, ensuring the online presence of the journal is top-notch.

Beyond the sphere of technology, Raiyan is a true sports enthusiast. He is a passionate fan of football, basketball, and soccer, cheering on his favorite teams - the Golden State Warriors in the NBA, the Kansas City Chiefs in the NFL, and Real Madrid in European football. His love for CAD modeling complements his enjoyment of sports and reflects in the strategic approach he applies to the video games he relishes playing. Raiyan Zaman is an amalgam of technological prowess, leadership ability, and diverse interests, continuously striving for personal and academic growth.

Leo Chen, Marketing Lead

Leo Chen is a dynamic sophomore at Centennial High School in Ellicott City, known for his dual passion for arts and academics. His talent in playing the viola allows him to express himself through enchanting melodies, while his unyielding commitment to academic excellence fuels his insatiable curiosity and personal development. As a firm believer in a balanced lifestyle, he regularly visits the gym, maintaining physical vigor and mental acuity.

Apart from personal interests, Leo is a prominent member of the board of DesCo, a dedicated CAD club, and the Chess Club. These affiliations not only stimulate his intellectual curiosity but also aid in refining his strategic and creative thinking skills. Leo is also deeply committed to making a positive impact on the world. He participates in various charitable endeavors, consistently striving to be a positive force in society.

Amidst his busy schedule, Leo finds relaxation and entertainment in video games, particularly Valorant, and immerses himself in the music of his favorite artist, Joji. A testament to his cultural appreciation, Leo has a profound connection with his Chinese heritage, a connection nurtured through his attendance at Chinese school. Embracing its traditions and values with immense pride and enthusiasm, Leo uses this cultural wealth to enrich his worldview and interpersonal relations.

Overall, Leo Chen is a well-rounded individual striving for positive change in the world, deriving fulfillment from various artistic, intellectual, and cultural pursuits.
