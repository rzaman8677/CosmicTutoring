How to Register

We are fully committed to helping students achieve their educational goals. Our tutoring services are absolutely free because we believe in equal access to education for all. Here are the steps to sign up:

  1. 01

    Sign up Through Google Forms

    Begin by filling out our Google Forms application to get your information into our database and start the process. It's easy and straightforward!

    Google Forms

  2. 02

    Receive a Confirmation Email

    Once your application is submitted, you will receive a confirmation email from contact@cosmictutoring.org. This email will verify your successful registration.

  3. 03

    Get in Touch with Your Tutor

    Following your registration confirmation, your assigned tutor will contact you. They will provide a Zoom link for your sessions, a pretest to assess your current skills, and a brief introduction of themselves.

  4. 04

    Engage in Tutoring Sessions

    Now, the learning begins! You'll engage in tutoring sessions with your assigned tutor until you're satisfied with your progress. Afterward, you're welcome to register for additional sessions or sign up for a new class.
